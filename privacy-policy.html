Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

1. Introduction

GCSC Smart Contractor ("GCSC", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform, website, and services (collectively, the "Services"). By using our Services, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

This policy complies with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and Washington State privacy requirements. If you are located in the European Union, additional GDPR protections may apply.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, mailing address, profile photo.
  • Contractor Verification: License number, license type, bond information, insurance policy details, certificate images. We do not collect Social Security Numbers (SSN) or full bank account numbers directly.
  • Project Information: Project descriptions, photos, budgets, addresses, and communications between Clients and Contractors.
  • Payment Information: We do not store credit card numbers or bank account details. All payment data is handled by our licensed payment processors (Stripe, Inc.).

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Log Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, access times, pages viewed, and referring URLs.
  • Cookies & Similar Technologies: We use cookies for authentication, security, analytics, and user preferences. You can control cookies through your browser settings.
  • Blockchain Data: When using XPR Network escrow, public wallet addresses and transaction hashes are recorded on the public blockchain. We cannot delete or modify blockchain records.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Provide Services: Match Clients with Contractors, process escrow payments, facilitate project communication.
  • Verify Identity: Validate contractor licenses, prevent fraud, ensure platform safety.
  • Process Payments: Coordinate with Stripe to handle deposits, milestone releases, and payouts.
  • Communications: Send service notifications, OTP codes, project updates, and marketing emails (which you can opt out of).
  • Improve Platform: Analyze usage patterns, fix bugs, develop new features.
  • Legal Compliance: Meet regulatory obligations, respond to legal requests, enforce our Terms of Service.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With Other Users: When you engage in a project, your profile information (name, photo, license number for Contractors) is visible to the other party.
  • Service Providers: Stripe (payments), XPR Network (blockchain escrow), hosting providers, email delivery services, analytics providers. All are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by subpoena, court order, or other legal process, or to protect our rights, property, or safety.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred as a business asset.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit.
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt.
  • JWT tokens with short expiration for authentication.
  • Regular security audits and automated vulnerability scanning.
  • Access controls and role-based permissions.

No system is 100% secure. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and for any activity under your account.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information as long as your account is active or as needed to provide Services. After account closure:

  • Account data is retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance.
  • Project and payment records are retained for 7 years per IRS requirements.
  • Chat messages are retained for 3 years after project completion.
  • Blockchain transaction data is permanent and cannot be deleted.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information through your profile settings.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@gcsc.store.

8. Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential: Required for authentication and security.
  • Functional: Remember your preferences and settings.
  • Analytics: Help us understand how users interact with the Platform (Google Analytics / Plausible).

You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings. Essential cookies cannot be disabled without affecting platform functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

10. Third-Party Links

The Platform may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., Washington L&I, Stripe). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be notified via email or a prominent notice on the Platform at least 30 days before taking effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.
